## Client Profile

  The Wizard's cart is a local book and game supplies store in Dayton, OH that sells comic books, TTRPG and TCG supplies, and hosts open house games and tournaments. The local business has recently overhauled their entire brand and want their new website to reflect that change. Their vision for the new website is a fun, playful, and maybe even fantasy-esque website design to sell products and advertise their events.


## Purpose & Goals

  The main purpose of this new website is to provide an easy method of browsing items for sale, purchasing those items from home, and being able to easily learn more about upcoming events. The website itself is focused around browsing and selling products, but there will also be sale advertisements and promotions for the store's game nights and other events.

  Their previous website was functional, but had no consistent color scheme or layout that many customers complained about. Their online sales have been lacking recently because of this, so success for the client looks like having an aesthetically pleasing, well organized, but still functional website. This way the user experience is much smoother and possibly facilitates more online purchases.


## Target Audience

#### Who will visit?
   There will likely be a very wide range of customers. From people who've never picked up a comic book or playing card, to those who are casual enjoyers, to some collectors looking for specific comic book issues or competitive players looking for certain supplies. Regardless of the customer's prior knowledge, it should be easy for all to be able to navigate the website.
  
#### Needs, habits, motivations
   The majority of people who either read comic books or play TTRPS/TCG games usually have an interest in both. Many TCG players struggle to find others to play against, which is why the store hosts tournaments and casual play. Same with the TTRPG players.

#### Tone, layout, color scheme appeal to them
   More playful and fun tone. The layout will be in sections so those who are looking for comic books and manga, gaming supplies, or to see the events going on can easily navigate to pages containing the relevant information. The color scheme is going to be similar to comic books in some way, consistent, and the fonts will likely be easy-to-read for the product descriptions but have a more fantasy feel for the big title pages.

  
## User Story or Journey

  A first-time visitor land on the homepage, then clicks through the various pages dedicated to comics, gaming supplies, and the event information/ registrations to find what they are interested in. After browsing and adding a product or two to their cart, the user goes to the checkout to complete their purchase.

  The aesthetic will be in the background and visually pleasing to the eye, but how the website is organized will be focused on making it intuitive and easy to understand for anyone.
